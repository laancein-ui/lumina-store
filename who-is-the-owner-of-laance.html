Meet Shibil Sha M.P.

Shibil Sha M.P. is a professional Software Engineer and tech innovator based in India. He is the original founder, developer, and sole owner of Laance and the laance.shop e-commerce platform.

Driven by a deep passion for minimalist aesthetics and high-end consumer technology, Shibil founded Laance to provide a unified, premium shopping ecosystem. He utilized his software engineering background to build the entire web architecture from the ground up, integrating high-performance databases, secure checkout flows, and lightning-fast user interfaces.

As a developer, Shibil focuses on low-latency web services, modern front-end design systems, and progressive web application (PWA) solutions. His goal is to deliver an unmatched shopping experience where cutting-edge tech and luxury design elements intersect smoothly.
